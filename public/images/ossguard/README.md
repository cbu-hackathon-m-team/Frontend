# Landing assets

- `comparison-*.jpg` and `build-*.jpg`: captures of OSSGuard's local Python demo at `/dashboard?project=python-uv-demo&tab=results`, in light and dark themes. They show example data, not production execution evidence.
- `python.svg`, `uv.svg`, `github.svg`, `docker.svg`: brand glyphs from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), retrieved 2026-10-31. Simple Icons is distributed under CC0-1.0; brand names and marks belong to their owners.
- Product diagrams are rendered by `src/pages/landing-product-artwork.tsx` with shared design tokens. Their compositions reference the product UI crops in [Framer](https://www.framer.com/), the connection diagrams in [Neon](https://neon.com/), and the container illustration in [Vercel](https://vercel.com/home). No reference-site screenshots or artwork are shipped as product images.
